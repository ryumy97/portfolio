This is the book that I've studied the basics of animations in code since from 2021.

## About

"The goal of this book is simple. We want to take a look at something that naturally occurs in our physical world, then determine how we can write code to simulate that occurence."

I believe the importance of this statement in the book is due to the fact - in human eyes, the resemblance of physical world is essential to making a good crafted animations and transitions, as that would be what humans (we) are most used to - like gravity, inertia, friction, and momentum. We experience them every single second.
When a digital animation of ui transition mimics these real-world mechanics, it reduces cognitive load, feels intuitive, and creates an immediate emotional connection.
